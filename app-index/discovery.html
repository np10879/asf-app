Executive Summary

Explore how research signals, testable hypotheses, and shared learning could inform new opportunities. Discovery is used here as a working label for research and exploration, not as a verified industry definition. The final spotlight must confirm its intended audience and scope with an accountable owner.

Key Notes & Updates

  • Research Signals: Proposed angle: bring together approved interviews, desk research, and observed needs while distinguishing evidence from assumptions.
  • Hypothesis Validation: Proposed angle: use small, reviewable experiments with clear questions and decision criteria. Do not imply that any experiment has already succeeded.
  • Knowledge Synthesis: Proposed angle: make findings, limitations, and open questions easier to reuse. Link to approved research artifacts before reporting conclusions.
